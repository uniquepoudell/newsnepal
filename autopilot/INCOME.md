# Income plan

The honest starting point: the site is new, so traffic is near zero and it earns nothing yet. Income comes from
**readers first**, then from turning readers into money. Every item below says what Claude can do alone and what
only the owner can do (signups and payouts must be in the owner's name).

| # | Income source | Status | Claude does | Owner does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Direct support (Stripe) | Live on Support button since 2026-11-26 | Places the button well, thanks page | Nothing |
| 2 | Email digest + sponsors in it | Planned (ROADMAP 3) | Builds digest and signup box | Picks provider and signs up (free) |
| 3 | Remittance affiliate links (Remitly, Wise) via GorkhaPay | GorkhaPay applying; News Nepal `rates.html` sends senders to the GorkhaPay comparison since 2026-11-26 | Adds links where money transfer fits | Signs up to the partner programs |
| 4 | Display ads (Google AdSense) | Waiting on own domain | `adsense` id already supported in config | Gets the domain, applies to AdSense |
| 5 | Sponsored cards and "Advertise" page | Planned (ROADMAP 8) | Builds slot and page | Agrees deals, sends Stripe invoices |
| 6 | Paid checklists (GorkhaPay products) | On sale at GorkhaPay | Links to them from visa stories | Nothing |
| 7 | Crypto donations | Live | Keeps the box tidy | Nothing |

Rejected: crypto exchange or trading referrals (illegal in Nepal), paid visa advice (needs MARA registration),
selling readers' data, spammy popups.

## Waiting on owner (in order of value)
1. Buy and point a normal domain (.com or .com.np) at the site: see `DOMAIN.md`. Needed for AdSense and SEO.
2. Pick an email provider and add its signup form id when ROADMAP 3 ships.
3. Sign up to Remitly and Wise partner programs (also helps GorkhaPay).
4. Optional: `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` repo secret for Nepali translations.
