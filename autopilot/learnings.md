# Learnings (newest at the top)

- 2026-11-26: Pages in a subfolder (`rates/2026-11-26.html`) would break the site's relative CSS/JS/nav links; use flat names (`rates-2026-11-26.html`). Branch names `autopilot/news-<date>` can clash with a same-day run: add a suffix.
- 2026-11-26: Google results for "AUD to NPR today" are all big converters (Wise, XE, Instarem, Paytm), many with one URL per amount (`?amount=10`). We won't outrank them in English; our edge is the official NRB rate, Nepali-language pages and Gulf/Malaysia corridors. Prefer Nepali titles and dated daily pages.
